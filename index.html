Founder Story

Hi, I'm Sarveshwar Mandal.

I believe the biggest opportunity of our generation is helping businesses transform with AI.

Over the past several years, I've worked across enterprise organizations and startups, building solutions in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Computer Vision, and Product Development. My experience spans global organizations such as Walmart Labs and Morgan Stanley, as well as leadership roles in fast-moving startups where execution, innovation, and business impact matter every day.

What I discovered is that most businesses don't have a technology problem—they have an execution problem. They know AI is important, but they struggle to identify the right opportunities, build the right systems, and turn ideas into measurable results.

That's why I founded my consulting practice and built products like ShadowAgent and Optivra: to help founders and organizations move from AI curiosity to AI-powered growth.

Today, I work with startups, SMEs, and growing companies to automate operations, unlock insights from data, develop intelligent products, and create scalable competitive advantages through AI.

My mission is simple:

Build practical AI solutions that generate real business outcomes—not just impressive demos.

If you're building something ambitious, let's create it together.

Why Work With Me Instead of Just Using AI Tools?

AI tools like ChatGPT are powerful. I use them every day. But AI tools alone don't create business outcomes. Implementation does.

AI Tools Give Answers

  • Generate ideas, content, and code snippets.
  • Help you explore possibilities.
  • Provide recommendations and templates.
  • Require you to connect systems and workflows yourself.
  • Leave deployment, monitoring, and maintenance up to you.
  • Don't take responsibility for business results.

I Build Business-Ready AI

  • Design and implement AI systems tailored to your business.
  • Integrate AI seamlessly with your existing tools, processes, and data.
  • Automate workflows to reduce manual effort and operational costs.
  • Deploy secure, scalable, and production-ready solutions.
  • Monitor performance, optimize results, and ensure reliability.
  • Measure ROI so you can clearly see the impact on revenue and growth.

AI tools provide intelligence.

I turn that intelligence into systems that generate measurable business value.

Whether you need AI automation, predictive analytics, intelligent assistants, computer vision solutions, or a complete AI-powered product, my focus is simple:

Building solutions that deliver real outcomes—not just suggestions.
